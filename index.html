About Kainat Tutorials

Clarity is a skill.

Kainat Tutorials is focused on one subject: Mathematics. Our aim is to help learners develop a stronger grasp of concepts through guided teaching, meaningful practice and steady support—online and offline.

Good to know

Questions,
answered.

Need something else? Send us an enquiry and we'll help you find the right information.

Which boards do you cover?+

We aim to support students across all boards. Share your board, class and syllabus during enquiry so we can confirm the relevant learning option.

Are classes online or offline?+

Both online and offline learning options are planned. Availability, location and timings are confirmed when you enquire.

Can I choose one-to-one or group tuition?+

Yes. You can enquire about individual tuition or group learning. We'll discuss the format that best fits your requirements.

Can I buy recorded courses or PDFs?+

Recorded courses and study materials are part of the planned learning catalogue. Product details, prices and access instructions will be shared when available.

How do I get started?+

Use the enquiry form below and tell us your class, board and preferred learning format. We'll follow up using the contact details you provide.
